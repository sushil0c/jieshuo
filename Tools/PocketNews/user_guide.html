Section 01

Getting Started

Welcome to PocketNews. This app brings news from multiple languages, categories and sources into one clean, simple screen. It works without an account, shows no ads, and stores everything on your device.

The first time you open PocketNews, a Welcome screen appears showing this user guide. Read through it and tap I Understand — Get Started to begin. This screen only appears once.

After dismissing the welcome screen, PocketNews automatically loads the latest news in the Health category in English. You can change the category and language immediately using the selectors at the top of the screen.

Good to know PocketNews works best with an internet connection for loading fresh news. However, articles you save offline and your news history are always available even without internet.

Section 03

Reading News

Opening an Article

Tap any article title in the news list to open it in reader view.

Changing Category or Language

Tap the left selector for category or right selector for language. The list refreshes immediately. Default categories are Health, Tech, and World. Default languages are English, Hindi, and Nepali.

Searching the Current List

Type in the search bar — results filter instantly. For example, typing budget shows only articles with that word in their title.

Press and hold any article title to see:

Section 06

AI Summarize

Uses Google Gemini AI to produce a short, clear summary of the article's main points.

How to Summarize

  1. Open any article in reader view
  2. Wait for the content to fully load
  3. Tap Sum. at the bottom
  4. The article text is replaced with the AI summary
Requirement You need a free Gemini API key in Settings. Visit aistudio.google.com/app/apikey, sign in with any Google account, create a key, and paste it in Settings under Gemini API Key.

How to Translate

  1. Open any article in reader view
  2. Wait for the content to fully load

Section 08

Paragraph Mode

Each paragraph is shown as its own block, separated by divider lines. Particularly helpful for screen reader users.

Why Use It

In standard full mode, all text is in one large area — a screen reader reads it all without stopping. In paragraph mode, each block is a separate element that a screen reader can announce individually and clearly.

Turning On Paragraph Mode

  1. Tap Set to open Settings
  2. Find Reader Mode and select paragraph
  3. Tap Save and Apply

To switch back, set Reader Mode to full.

Section 10

Offline Reading

Save the full content of any article so you can read it without internet.

Saving an Article Offline

Press and hold any article, then tap Save Offline. A confirmation appears when saving completes.

Auto-Save

PocketNews automatically saves the top 5 articles each time the news loads — silently in the background. The auto-save skips articles already saved so storage stays clean.

Reading Offline Articles

Tap [S] Offline in the bottom navigation bar. Tap any article to read it instantly.

Deleting a Single Offline Article

Tap [S] Offline, then long press any article in the list. A confirmation dialog appears — tap Delete to remove just that one article.

Deleting All Offline Articles

Inside Offline, tap Delete All at the bottom, or go to Settings → Data → Clear All Offline News.

Section 13

Adding Custom News Sources

PocketNews supports any website with an RSS feed. RSS feed URLs usually look like:

https://example.com/feed
https://example.com/rss
https://example.com/news.xml

Adding a Feed

  1. Tap [+] Add Feed in the bottom navigation bar
  2. Paste the RSS feed URL into the text field
  3. Choose the category and language
  4. Tap Add
Tip Use Feed Health Checker in Settings to test a URL before adding it. This avoids broken or wrong feeds.

Managing & Removing Feeds

Tap [+] Add Feed in the bottom nav to open the Manage Feeds screen. All your custom feeds are listed by domain, category and language. Tap any feed to see its full URL and remove it individually. You can also go to Settings → Feed Tools → Manage Feeds (Add / Remove).

Note Removing a feed only deletes it from your custom list. Built-in default feeds are never affected.

Section 16

OPML Import

Import a full list of RSS feeds at once from another app like Feedly, Inoreader, or NewsBlur.

Getting Your OPML

  • Feedly — Profile → Import and Export → Export OPML
  • Inoreader — Preferences → Import and Export → Export OPML
  • NewsBlur — Account Settings → Download OPML

What OPML Looks Like

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<opml version="1.0">
  <head><title>My News Feeds</title></head>
  <body>
    <outline text="TechCrunch" type="rss"
      xmlUrl="https://feeds.feedburner.com/TechCrunch/"
      htmlUrl="https://techcrunch.com"/>

    <outline text="BBC World News" type="rss"
      xmlUrl="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/world/rss.xml"
      htmlUrl="https://www.bbc.com/news/world"/>

    <outline text="Nepali Times" type="rss"
      xmlUrl="https://nepalitimes.com/feed"
      htmlUrl="https://nepalitimes.com"/>

    <outline text="Kantipur Daily" type="rss"
      xmlUrl="https://ekantipur.com/rss"
      htmlUrl="https://ekantipur.com"/>

    <outline text="Setopati" type="rss"
      xmlUrl="https://www.setopati.com/feed"
      htmlUrl="https://www.setopati.com"/>
  </body>
</opml>

PocketNews reads only the xmlUrl value from each line — the actual RSS feed address.

How to Import

  1. Copy all the OPML content
  2. Go to Settings → Feed Tools → OPML Import
  3. Paste the content into the text box
  4. Select the Category and Language to import into
  5. Tap Import
Safe to repeat You can import the same OPML multiple times — duplicates are skipped automatically.

Section 19

Tips & Tricks

Read without distraction

Use Sepia theme with a comfortable font size. No ads, no pop-ups, no recommended content.

Stay informed offline

Open PocketNews on Wi-Fi. Auto-save stores the top 5 articles silently — ready when you go offline.

Find old articles fast

Tap History, select Last 28 days, and type a keyword. Find any article in seconds.

Add local news feeds

Tap [+] Add Feed to open Manage Feeds. Paste any RSS URL, pick category and language. Test with Feed Health Checker first.

Stay updated automatically

Set a notification interval in Settings so PocketNews delivers the latest headline to your notification panel every 30 minutes, hourly, or on your own schedule.

AI features are free

Get a free Gemini key at aistudio.google.com/app/apikey. Summarize and Translate both work instantly.

Use paragraph mode

If you use TalkBack or Jieshuo screen reader, switch to paragraph mode. Each block is announced separately.

Share and save together

Long press an article in the news list to bookmark, save offline, or share — all without opening it first.

Remove items individually

In Offline news, long press any saved article to delete just that one. In Bookmarks, long press any bookmark and tap Remove Bookmark.

Listen while you read

Open the Radio tab, tap a station to start playing, then press Back to return to the station list or tap Home to browse news — audio continues in the background.

Add your own radio stations

Tap + Add in the radio tab or go to Settings → Radio → Manage Radio Stations. You need the direct stream URL (usually ending in .mp3, .aac, or /stream).

Backup before changes

Export a backup before editing feeds or settings. Restore everything in seconds if needed.

Section 20

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the article showing a short description instead of full text?
Some websites block automatic content loading. Tap Browser at the bottom of the reader to open the full article in your device browser.
The news list is empty. What do I do?
Check your internet connection, then tap [H] Home to reload. Try switching category or language. If still empty, add a custom feed using Add Feed.
Summarize and Translate are not working.
You need a Gemini API key in Settings. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and paste it under Gemini API Key.
I added a custom RSS feed but see no articles from it.
Check that you selected the correct category and language when adding the feed. The feed only appears when that combination is selected on the home screen.
How do I remove a single bookmark?
Go to [B] Saved, then long press the bookmark you want to remove and tap Remove Bookmark.
How do I delete a single offline article?
Tap [S] Offline, then long press any article in the list. Tap Delete in the confirmation dialog.
How do I remove a specific RSS feed I added?
Tap [+] Add Feed in the bottom nav to open Manage Feeds. Tap the feed you want to remove and tap Remove.
Is my data sent anywhere?
Everything is stored only on your device. The only exception is when you use Summarize or Translate — those send the article text to Google Gemini AI for processing.
How often does the news refresh?
News refreshes every time you tap [H] Home or change the category or language. There is no automatic background refresh.
Can I use PocketNews without a Google account?
Yes. PocketNews works fully without any account. A Google account is only needed for the free Gemini AI features.
The app opened an article but the content does not match the title.
This can happen when an RSS feed contains mixed content or outdated links. Tap Browser to open the original article.
A radio station shows an error when I try to play it.
The station's stream URL may be offline or changed. Try another station. For custom stations, verify the stream URL is still active using a browser or media player on your device.
How do I stop the radio?
In the player view, tap the ⏹ Stop button in the bottom nav bar. Or press Back — the radio stops automatically when you leave the player.
Can I listen to radio while reading news?
Not simultaneously — the radio stops when you leave the player view. To read and listen at the same time, use your device's built-in media player for the radio stream and PocketNews for news.

Radio Player

PocketNews includes a built-in live radio player with over 200 stations from Nepal and around the world, plus support for your own custom stream URLs.

Opening the Radio Tab

  1. Tap [R] Radio in the bottom navigation bar
  2. The app loads the station list — built-in stations appear first, custom stations at the bottom
  3. Use the search bar at the top to filter by station name or location

Playing a Station

  1. Tap any station name in the list
  2. The Player View opens showing the station name, frequency, and location
  3. The app connects automatically — status shows ⏳ Connecting… then ▶ Now Playing

Radio Sleep Timer

The Sleep Timer lets you set a countdown after which the radio will automatically stop — useful when listening before sleep or during a break.

Setting the Timer

  1. Open the Radio tab and start playing a station
  2. In the player view, tap the ⏱ Sleep Timer: Off button
  3. Choose a duration: 15, 30, 60, or 90 minutes
  4. The label updates to show the remaining time, e.g. Stops in 30 min

Cancelling the Timer

Tap ⏱ again and choose Cancel Timer. The timer is also automatically cancelled if you close the player manually before it fires.

What happens when the timer fires The radio stops, the player closes, and a toast message confirms the action. No alarm sound is played.

No Internet Screen

When PocketNews cannot connect to any news source — for example when your device is offline or your connection is too slow — it shows a clear No Internet screen instead of leaving the feed empty or frozen.

What You See

A centred screen with a 📡 icon, the message "No Internet Connection", a short explanation, and a red ↻ Retry button.

Retrying

Tap Retry to try loading the news again. If your connection has been restored, the feed will load normally and the error screen will disappear. You can also tap the Home button in the navigation bar, which also triggers a reload.

Partial failures If some feeds load but others fail, the app shows whatever articles it received without displaying the error screen. The error screen only appears when all fetches fail.
